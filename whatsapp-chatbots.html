CHATBOT FAQ

Before we
automate.

How much does a WhatsApp chatbot cost?

All chatbot projects receive a custom quotation. Cost depends on the conversation flows, integrations, platform requirements, and ongoing services needed.

Can it connect to my booking or order system?

Potentially, if the system offers suitable access or an API and the integration is technically supported. We assess this before confirming scope.

Will a chatbot replace my staff?

The goal is to handle suitable repetitive interactions and make common information easier to access. Human handover remains important for complex requests and customer care.

Do I need WhatsApp Business Platform access?

Some automated use cases require access to Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform or an approved provider. Requirements depend on the proposed setup and current platform rules.
