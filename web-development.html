WEB DEVELOPMENT FAQ

Questions before
you get started.

How much does a website cost?

Proposed packages are $99, $299, and $650. Projects outside those scopes receive a custom quotation. See website pricing.

Will my website work on phones?

Responsive layouts are included in the proposed packages. We test common screen sizes and adjust the layout for usability.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain registration, hosting, paid plugins, subscriptions, maintenance, and third-party fees are not assumed to be included. Confirm the exact inclusions in your written project scope before proceeding.

Can you connect payments or bookings?

We can discuss supported options. Availability depends on the provider, technical access, integration requirements, and agreed scope.
